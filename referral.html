Customer programs

Rewards built around real value

01

Referral Rewards

Share Volunteer Mobile Auto Care & both of you save

Refer a friend, family member, coworker, or neighbor to Volunteer Mobile Auto Care. When they book a qualifying repair, they receive a discount and you earn a reward toward your next service.

New Customer $25 off their qualifying repair.
Referring Customer $25 off your next qualifying repair after the referred customer completes service.
02

VIP Rewards

Five oil changes. Your choice of reward.

Earn one VIP stamp with each qualifying oil change. After five stamps, choose one of the following rewards:

$25 Off $25 off your next qualifying service.
Free Tire Rotation One standard tire rotation at no charge.
Free Cabin Air Filter Installation Labor to install one cabin air filter at no charge. Filter is additional.
$50 Off $50 off a qualifying repair of $300 or more.
03

Community Discounts

Everyday savings for those we appreciate

Volunteer Mobile Auto Care offers ongoing discounts to qualifying customers as a small way of saying thank you for the work they do in our community.

Veterans & Active Military 10% off labor, up to $100, or $5 off an oil change.
First Responders 10% off labor, up to $100, or $5 off an oil change.
Educators 10% off labor, up to $100, or $5 off an oil change.
Seniors 65+ 10% off labor, up to $100, or $5 off an oil change.
04

Neighborhood Service Special

More vehicles. More savings.

Schedule multiple vehicles for service at the same location and during the same appointment window to save on labor.

2 Vehicles $10 off labor.
3 Vehicles $15 off labor.
4 Vehicles $20 off labor.

Unless otherwise stated, discounts, rewards, and promotional offers cannot be combined. One discount, reward, or promotional offer may be applied per service appointment. Additional exclusions may apply.
