About us

Auto care built around convenience, value, and community

Volunteer Mobile Auto Care was created to bring professional, affordable, and convenient vehicle services directly to East Tennessee drivers.

Our story

Service that supports the people we serve

We believe quality automotive care should be honest, accessible, and centered around the customer—not the repair shop.

We are also committed to giving back to the communities that support us. Through our discount programs, referral rewards, VIP Rewards, community giveaways, and our future Auto Care Relief Fund for qualified applicants, we hope to make dependable vehicle maintenance more accessible while building lasting relationships with the people we serve.

Our mission

Earn trust through every service call

Our mission is simple: provide dealership-quality service with the convenience of a mobile mechanic while treating every customer with honesty, respect, and transparency. We measure our success not only by the repairs we complete, but by the trust we earn and the positive impact we have in our community.
