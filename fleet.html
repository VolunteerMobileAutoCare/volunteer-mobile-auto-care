Fleet Service

Keep your fleet moving with on-site service

Reduce downtime and keep your vehicles on the road with professional maintenance, diagnostics, and repair performed at your business or other approved service location.

Built for small & light-duty fleets

Volunteer Mobile Auto Care helps local businesses keep work vehicles maintained without sending employees across town to a repair shop. On-site service can make routine maintenance easier to schedule and help reduce unnecessary vehicle downtime.

  • Service vans and light-duty trucks
  • Contractor and trade vehicles
  • Property-management fleets
  • Delivery and local business vehicles
  • Small municipal and nonprofit fleets

Common fleet services

  • Oil and filter service
  • Brake service and inspections
  • Battery, starting, and charging-system service
  • Warning-light and drivability diagnostics
  • Cooling-system service and repair
  • Steering and suspension repairs
  • Routine vehicle inspections
  • Preventive maintenance scheduling
  • Maintenance documentation

Fleet Advantages

Less downtime. More time on the road.

On-site convenience Service can be performed at your business or other approved location, reducing unnecessary trips to a repair shop.
Reduced downtime Keep employees and work vehicles available by scheduling maintenance around your normal operations whenever possible.
Preventive maintenance Routine inspections and scheduled service can help identify concerns before they become larger repairs or unexpected breakdowns.
Clear documentation Receive straightforward service records, findings, and recommendations to help you stay on top of fleet maintenance.
