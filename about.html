About us

Auto care built around convenience and trust

Volunteer Mobile Auto Care was created to make professional vehicle service easier for East Tennessee drivers.

Our approach

We bring the tools, diagnostics, and service process to your location. Before work begins, we explain the concern, the recommended repair, and the expected cost.

Our goal is not to sell every possible repair. It is to help you understand what is urgent, what should be planned, and what is currently operating as intended.
