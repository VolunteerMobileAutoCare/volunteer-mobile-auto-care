About us

Auto care built around convenience, value, and community

Volunteer Mobile Auto Care provides professional mobile automotive maintenance, diagnostics, and repair throughout East Tennessee. Our goal is simple: deliver dependable service, clear communication, and transparent pricing while bringing the convenience of a repair shop directly to you.

Our approach

Service that supports the people we serve

We believe quality automotive care should be honest, accessible, and centered around the customer—not the repair shop.

We are also committed to giving back to the communities that support us. Through our discount programs, referral rewards, VIP Rewards, community giveaways, and our Auto Care Relief Fund, which helps support qualifying local residents with needed vehicle repairs, we hope to make dependable vehicle maintenance more accessible while building lasting relationships with the people we serve.

Professional ASE-certified service, professional-grade equipment, proper repair procedures, and a 12-month / 12,000-mile warranty on qualifying repairs.
Transparent Clear estimates, straightforward pricing, and recommendations explained before additional work begins.
Convenient Professional automotive service brought directly to your home, workplace, or other approved location.
Community-focused Discounts, rewards, community partnerships, giveaways, and support through the Auto Care Relief Fund.

Professional Standards

ASE Certified & Professionally Equipped

Volunteer Mobile Auto Care is ASE certified and committed to accurate diagnostics, proper repair procedures, and continued automotive education. Professional-grade tools and equipment are used to help identify problems correctly and complete repairs with the care your vehicle deserves.

Our mission

Earn trust through every service call

Our mission is to provide dependable, professional automotive service while treating every customer with honesty, respect, and transparency. We aim to earn trust through accurate work, clear communication, and recommendations based on what your vehicle actually needs—not unnecessary repairs.

What to expect

A simple service process

  1. Request service Send us your vehicle information, location, and a description of the concern.
  2. Review & estimate We review the request, confirm availability, and discuss expected pricing before scheduling.
  3. Mobile appointment We arrive prepared for the approved service at your home, workplace, or other approved location.
  4. Results & recommendations You receive clear findings, completed-service details, and any recommended next steps.
